3D anatomy in this app is a simplified derivative of the Human Reference Atlas
united male reference organ set, version 1.10.

Source: HuBMAP / Human Reference Atlas, 3D Reference Object Library
https://humanatlas.io/3d-reference-library
DOI: https://doi.org/10.48539/hbm833.wzwn.425

The reference was built from Visible Human Male data (U.S. National Library of
Medicine; Spitzer et al. 1996; Ackerman 1998) and includes Allen brain
structures and other contributed organ sets distributed with the HRA release.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The mesh was decimated for a phone-sized real-time view. It is an anatomical
reference, not a patient scan and not a diagnosis.

Changes: meshes were grouped into 13 body systems, tiny structures that do not
read on a phone were omitted, and geometry was simplified and quantized.
